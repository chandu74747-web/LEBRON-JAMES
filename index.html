About LeBron

LeBron Raymone James was born on December 30, 1984, in Akron, Ohio. A star at St. Vincent–St. Mary High School, he was picked first overall by the Cleveland Cavaliers in the 2003 NBA Draft.

A forward who plays with a point guard's vision, he has won championships with three different franchises and is the only player to pass 40,000 career points.

Off the court, he founded the I PROMISE School in Akron, which supports local students and families.
